Scope: every app screen of Kafila (home/join, trip shell + Trip, Plan, Decide, Money, Jar, Wrapped tabs). Mode: Operate. Audience: Indian friend groups 18–30 on phones, often outdoors in daylight; judges watching a desktop demo. Primary task on the lead surface: make and read the fair plan.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The trip is a hand-painted goods truck, built by many hands — one painted board per screen carries the verdict, everything else is a clean working app. Refuses the category's white-card-and-teal travel template and its dark-neon opposite.

OWN-WORLD: Lime-wash ground #F4F6F1, deep ink #1A1410, chrome yellow #F2B705, truck red #C8102E, parrot green #1E8C45 (the only live/selected colour). Each tab owns one paint (Trip yellow, Plan green, Decide red, Money ink) as a 4px band under the app bar and its tab indicator. Bungee for the one painted board per screen; Hind for everything else. Fairness and progress drawn as reflector tape (red/white segments in an ink-outlined track) on one fixed length scale; the board has a red/green chevron border band.

STORY: A friend opens the trip link, sees who's in, decides by liking parts of options, presses "Make a fair plan" and instantly reads "Fits everyone's budget" plus why it works for them — without anyone's budget exposed. Then splits money and saves in the Jar.
